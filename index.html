Software engineer for interactive systems — from 8 KB of SRAM to Unreal Engine 5.

I build museum exhibits, expo applications and augmented reality — apps that let a visitor hold up an iPad and see virtual elements placed in the real room. Unity and Unreal on the surface, Arduino and Raspberry Pi boards, sensors and LED controllers underneath. My background is electrical engineering, which is why my own projects run further down the stack: renderers and firmware written from scratch.
